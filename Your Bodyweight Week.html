Post-workout power bowl

Lentil, egg and avocado bowl · about 10 min with pre-cooked lentils
~35 g protein~18 g fiber~550 kcal
  • ¾ cup cooked lentils (canned works; rinse well)
  • 2 eggs, boiled or fried
  • 2 handfuls of spinach, wilted or raw
  • ½ avocado, sliced
  • A handful of cherry tomatoes
  • ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt, as a creamy topping
  • Lemon juice, salt, pepper, a pinch of cumin

Warm the lentils with the spices, pile on the spinach, then top with the eggs, avocado, tomatoes and a spoonful of yogurt. Finish with lemon.

Swaps: firm tofu or canned tuna for the eggs; chickpeas or black beans for the lentils. Numbers are rough estimates and vary by brand and portion size. Eat it within a couple of hours after training.

Leave 1–2 rest days between strength sessions (Mon / Tue / Thu / Sat works well). A brisk 20-minute walk on any other day is a good bonus. Move in a range that feels comfortable and stop if anything hurts sharply. If you have an injury or a health condition, check with a clinician first.
